Stream Entry

Stream entry is the first irreversible stage of awakening. The yogi who attains it has crossed a threshold that cannot be uncrossed. Something fundamental in the relationship between the mind and its own experience has permanently changed.

It is attained through sustained practice with the Progress of Insight — sixteen insight knowledges moving from early concentration through the arising and passing event, dissolution, the dukkha ñāṇas of fear, misery and re-observation, and into equanimity, where the path event becomes possible.

At stream entry, three things are permanently ended:

  • Identity view — the deep-seated sense that there is a fixed, separate self at the centre of experience
  • Doubt — uncertainty about whether the path leads where it is said to lead
  • Attachment to rites and rituals — the belief that the form of practice, rather than its substance, is what liberates

What remains is a practitioner who knows, from direct experience, that the project is real. The first crossing is the decisive one.

The Resolution

Making a firm resolution for stream entry is itself a practice. It gives the mind direction. It creates a context for every sit. When motivation flags — and it will — the resolution reminds the mind of what it has already committed to.

The resolution is made, then released. You state it clearly, let it settle, and return to the practice. The mind now has a vector.

Your resolution
"Resolve with all your mental energy to attain stream entry. Do this again and again. Incline your mind however best you can to that again and again without restraint. Total, natural, wonderful commitment is key."
"Resolving for something seems to push the mind in the desired direction. The fact that the mind has resolved to accomplish something is enough for it to start doing what needs to be done — or to keep the momentum going until it does."
"I resolve with all my mental focus to experience something, then I let the resolution go. The mind does the rest."
"It gives you permission to get it done. It creates a very conducive mindset. If you start to slacken off, the resolve will arise in the mind to remind you — note that slacking off and get back on track."
